# Locked decision ledger

This ledger preserves owner decisions from the 2026-08-26 ByteDesk family design
session. It is explanatory evidence; root and profile design contracts are normative.

## Family

- Use `reference-dark-primary.png` as the material and atmosphere authority for future
  ByteDesk applications.
- Black Glass + Optical Layering is the shared shell language.
- Electric blue and restrained orange are the family energy accents.
- Dark and light are exact counterparts, not independently composed themes.
- Product personality remains mandatory inside the shared system.
- Optical layering is the selected depth level; avoid both flatness and excessive 3D.
- Dark richness is user-adjustable through governed presets.
- Product icons embody their name/function through dimensional technical metaphors.

## Product identity and naming

- `bytedesk.ai` remains the website name.
- `Workforce` remains the product name.
- Store is expected to merge into the website. Retain its icon concept as migration
  evidence, but do not create a replacement Store name or new standalone identity.
- Agent Memory uses a brain/memory metaphor.
- Agent Browser uses an application/browser-shell metaphor.
- Capture uses a targeting/aperture metaphor.
- Agent Mail uses a routed envelope/network metaphor.
- Workforce uses a people/hierarchy metaphor.
- Marketplace Server uses a server/distribution-hub metaphor.
- The disk analyzer and SEO/marketing platform were to receive new names. The exact
  accepted winners are absent from the surviving transcript supplied to this update;
  do not reconstruct or invent them. Record them when the owner reconfirms.
- The early bytedesk.ai and Store concepts on `reference-product-icons.png` were
  rejected and are not approved marks.

## Delivery sequence

- Mock image first, then approved HTML, then native/runtime adoption.
- The orchestration interface is first, followed by Agent Mail, Capture, and remaining
  native/non-web applications; web applications and companion websites follow.
- Every page and state is mocked in HTML and every required reusable component exists
  in Storybook before adoption into existing development branches.
- The orchestration dashboard uses an assistant-style central journal with structural
  metadata to the left and live turn/evidence/decision metadata to the right.
- Full-screen orchestration targets 1600x900 primary and 1600x1200 expanded canvases,
  remains responsive, and keeps a floating shell rather than edge-to-edge tiling.

## AG-UI boundary

AG-UI is a portable client projection vocabulary, not the canonical durable record.
The orchestration journal remains authoritative. Adapters may project source-valid
events into AG-UI without inventing provider text, tool state, cost, context, retry, or
policy events that the engine does not expose.
