# Black Glass + Optical Layering

Canonical visual record for the shared ByteDesk application-shell language.

## Approval

- Owner: ByteDesk product owner
- Decision date: 2026-08-26
- State: approved direction with one open parity asset
- Supersedes: dark-only flat-shell guidance and generic SaaS/dashboard studies

The primary dark reference and the Black Glass + Optical Layering direction were
explicitly selected in the Toolbox design review. Dark/light parity, electric-blue and
restrained orange energy, product personality, anthropomorphic product marks, and a
user-adjustable dark-richness setting were also explicitly locked.

## Extracted material contract

Measured from the primary dark reference:

- source canvas: 995 x 1581 px;
- floating shell: approximately 855 x 1282 px, with a 12-14 px outer radius;
- dominant grounds: `#16191C`, `#181C21`, `#1C2228`, and `#272A2C`;
- primary ink: approximately `#E6E8EB`; secondary ink: approximately `#A1A8B1`;
- electric-blue raster clusters span approximately `#1475E1` to `#1B84F3`;
- shell insets and row rhythm resolve to an 8 px grid with common 16, 20, and 24 px gaps;
- structure: atmospheric canvas -> floating shell -> inset control planes -> selected or
  raised surface -> precisely bounded identity/activity glow;
- outer depth: broad blue-black ambient shadow, visually about 32-48 px of spread;
- borders: 1 px cool hairlines with a subtle inner top highlight.

Raster antialias colors are evidence, not additional tokens. Canonical values normalize
them into semantic families. The generated glyphs suggest a Sans + Mono system but do
not prove a font identity; IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono remain canonical.

## Use

- Treat the primary dark reference as the visual regression target for material,
  atmosphere, shell elevation, density, and technical character.
- Treat the parity board as evidence that light and dark keep identical composition.
- Rebuild controls, text, layout, borders, and shadows as live components and tokens.
  Do not ship full-page raster slices as UI.
- Product icons may borrow the dimensional metal/glass construction, blue energy, and
  orange core, but each mark still needs its own catalog approval.
- Never infer production download, install, entitlement, agent, or update behavior from
  these mockups.

## Rejected drift

- edge-to-edge flat panels that remove the atmospheric canvas and floating shell;
- generic observability dashboards, KPI tiles, topology graphs, and rainbow telemetry;
- frosted haze, excessive blur, nested glass cards, and dramatic 3D extrusion;
- dark and light images with different content, controls, or hierarchy;
- generic placeholder icons used as final product identity;
- invented runtime state presented as production capability.
